## Selected Electronics and Embedded Projects

### Capstone - Reflow Solder Surface

<img
  src="/resources/posts/academic-portfolio/capstone-table.jpg"
  alt="SMD reflow hot plate capstone project"
  class="half"
/>

Hot plate for SMD reflow. I led the core embedded design, covering MCU and peripheral selection as well as schematic and pinout layout for the PCB. From there, I implemented sensor fusion and the temperature control loop, and developed the user interface using an LCD and rotary encoder.

### H-Bridge and Stepper Motor Driver - Linear Power Supply

Two simple power electronics projects I did early in my studies. In both cases, I simulated the circuit in SPICE, prototyped the circuit on a breadboard, then manufactured it as a PCB.

- A dual H-Bridge with a push-pull FET driving circuit, used to drive a stepper motor.
- A dual rail power supply with a rectifier and two linear regulators for an at-home lab supply.
